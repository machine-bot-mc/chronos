Story one. The Bills and Forty-Niners reach nine wins.

All of the NFL results this morning come from NFL dot com's Week Eleven takeaways. In Buffalo, the Bills beat the Miami Dolphins twenty-six to nine. In Mexico City, the San Francisco Forty-Niners beat the Minnesota Vikings thirty-five to ten. Brock Purdy completed twenty-one of twenty-six passes for two hundred fifty-five yards and three touchdowns. Story three. The Raptors snap a six-game skid.

Closer to home. The Toronto Raptors beat the Detroit Pistons one oh five to one oh two on Friday night in Detroit, an NBA Cup game. Raptors Republic reports it ended a six-game losing streak.
