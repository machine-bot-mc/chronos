# Chronos weekly schedule
#
# One line per day:   Day | Topic | How far back to look | Notes
#
# Edit any line on GitHub (pencil icon, then "Commit changes"). The next morning uses it.
# - Topic and notes are plain English. Write them the way you'd brief a researcher.
# - "How far back to look" is how old a source may be. Examples: last 3 days, last 90 days,
#   last 12 months, any time.
# - Lines starting with # are ignored.
# - Delete this whole file to go back to personal AI every day.

Monday    | Professional sports  | last 3 days   | The weekend's results and biggest storylines. General coverage of the major North American leagues; only sports that are in season.
Tuesday   | Business             | last 90 days  | Companies, deals, strategy and market shifts that matter beyond the daily noise, with a Canadian angle where there is one. Developments, not stock tips.
Wednesday | Personal AI          | last 7 days   | Personal AI use cases around home, work and family: what automation opportunities are coming to light in daily life.
Thursday  | Health               | last 12 months | New research and what it actually shows: study size, whether it was in people, and how strong the evidence is. No personal medical advice.
Friday    | International news   | last 7 days   | The biggest world stories of the week, from wire services and established outlets, with more than one side where a story is contested.
Saturday  | Fascinating facts    | any time      | Three or four surprising, verified facts around one theme. Every fact traced to a reputable source. No popular myths.
Sunday    | Book reviews         | last 3 years  | One or two notable books: what they're about and what named critics said. Short quotes only, no spoilers, and never my own opinion dressed up as a review.
